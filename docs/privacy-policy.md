# Woodmark Youth — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

Woodmark Youth is the app for the youth group of Woodmark Church in Kirkland, Washington ("we", "us"). This page explains, in plain words, what we keep about you and what we don't.

## The short version

- We keep as little as possible: your **name**, your **email** (only to sign you in), and your **birthday** if you choose to add it (day and month only, no year).
- We **never** ask for your phone number, and there are **no passwords**.
- We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps.
- Anonymous questions really are anonymous: they are not connected to your account.
- You can delete your account at any time, right in the app.

## What we collect

| What | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Name | So people in the group know who you are | Members of the group |
| Email address | To send you a one-time sign-in code | Nobody in the app. Only our database provider and the app administrator. |
| Birthday (optional, day and month) | So the group can celebrate you | On your birthday, members see that it's your day. Leaders see the list of upcoming birthdays. |
| What you post: chat messages, comments, reactions, ideas, "I'm in" on events, photos and videos | This is what the app is for | Members of the group |
| Notification address of your phone (push token) | To send notifications you turned on | Nobody in the app |

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

## Anonymous questions

When you ask a leader an anonymous question:

- Your phone creates a secret key that stays **only on your phone**.
- The question is sent **without** your account, and our server stores only a scrambled fingerprint of that key. There is no column in our database that says who asked.
- All leaders can read and answer the question. You see the name of the leader who answers.
- The conversation is deleted automatically **7 days after the last message**, or immediately when you tap **Delete**.
- Because the key lives only on your phone, switching phones or deleting the app means you lose access to that conversation.

Like every internet service, our hosting provider briefly keeps technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security. These logs are not linked to your account or to your question.

If a message suggests that someone is in danger, leaders may act on what was written, but they still cannot see who wrote it.

## Who we share information with

Only the services that run the app:

- **Supabase** — stores the database and files (United States).
- **Expo, Apple and Google** — deliver push notifications to your phone.
- **Resend** — sends the sign-in code to your email.

We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

## Staying safe

- You can **report** any message, comment or idea. Leaders review reports.
- You can **block** a person, and you will stop seeing what they post.
- Leaders can remove content that breaks the group's rules.

## Deleting your information

- In the app: **Me → Delete account**. Your profile, messages, comments, ideas and reactions are removed right away.
- Can't open the app? Email hello@woodmarkyouth.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account within 30 days.
- Photos and events that leaders posted for the whole group stay, without your account attached.
- If the app administrator removes someone from the group for breaking the rules, that account and its content are deleted too. We keep only a scrambled fingerprint of that email address, so the same address can't join again. We can't turn the fingerprint back into the address.

## Children and teens

This app is only for people who are **13 or older**. You confirm your age when you join, and we keep the date you confirmed it and agreed to the group rules.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we delete it. If you think a child under 13 is using the app, email hello@woodmarkyouth.com and we will take care of it.

## Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and tell you in the app.
